About the Event

This coding event is held at Universiti Malaysia Terengganu on the east coast of Malaysia. It gives students the opportunity to enjoy the beautiful Terengganu seaside while improving their coding skills and competing with other programmers.

Programming Workshop

Learn basic programming skills from experienced lecturers.

Mini Hackathon

Work together with other students to solve problems.

Career Talk

Learn about future career opportunities in technology.
