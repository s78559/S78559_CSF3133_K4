About UMT

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. It is well known for its specialisation in marine science, aquatic resources and environmental studies.

The university provides various academic programmes in science, technology, management and other fields. UMT aims to produce knowledgeable and competent graduates who contribute to society and environmental sustainability.

With its strategic location near the South China Sea, UMT offers opportunities for students and researchers to explore marine ecosystems, fisheries and environmental conservation.

History of UMT

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu began as the Centre for Fisheries and Marine Science under Universiti Pertanian Malaysia. It was established to provide training facilities for students and research facilities for lecturers.

In June 1996, the institution became Universiti Pertanian Malaysia Terengganu (UPMT). It was later approved as Kolej Universiti Terengganu (KUT) in 1999 and renamed Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia (KUSTEM) in 2001.

On 1 February 2007, KUSTEM was upgraded to a full-fledged university and officially became Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT).
