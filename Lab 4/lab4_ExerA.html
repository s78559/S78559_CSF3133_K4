Welcome to Terengganu

Terengganu is one of the most beautiful states in Malaysia. It is famous for its beautiful islands, traditional culture, delicious local food and stunning natural attractions. Whether you enjoy relaxing at the beach, exploring historical places or experiencing nature, Terengganu has something special for everyone.

Popular Tourist Destinations

Pulau Redang

Pulau Redang is famous for its crystal-clear seawater, white sandy beaches and beautiful coral reefs. Visitors can enjoy snorkelling, diving and relaxing by the beach while enjoying the tropical scenery.

Promotion Price: RM299 RM199

Masjid Kristal

Masjid Kristal is a famous landmark located in Kuala Terengganu. Its unique architecture combines glass, steel and crystal, creating a beautiful appearance especially when illuminated at night.

Promotion Price: RM99 RM59

Tasik Kenyir

Tasik Kenyir is the largest man-made lake in Southeast Asia. It offers beautiful natural scenery, waterfalls, fishing activities and exciting adventures for nature lovers.

Promotion Price: RM250 RM180
